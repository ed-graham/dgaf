Other consultancies care deeply.

They explore.

They facilitate.

They immerse themselves in your organisation.

We maintain a healthier emotional distance.

The Minimum Viable Fück™ is the smallest amount of care required to produce something that passes casual inspection.

No more.

Occasionally less.
